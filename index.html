Common questions

Before you buy

How much range does an e-rickshaw provide?

Range depends on the exact model, battery, load, route, speed, weather and battery condition. Ask for the current variant's tested range and test conditions.

How long does charging take?

Charging time depends on battery capacity, chemistry, charger and state of charge. Confirm the approved charger and exact charging time for your selected variant.

Can I enquire about finance or EMI?

You can use the indicative calculator above, then contact the company or an authorised finance provider to confirm eligibility, interest, fees and final terms.

Are the listed prices final?

No. Online/sample prices are indicative and may change by model, variant, location, tax, registration, insurance and stock. Request a written quotation.

Can I become a dealer or order multiple vehicles?

Select dealership or bulk purchase in the enquiry form and include your city, expected quantity and business details.
